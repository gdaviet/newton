Add an experimental `"RT1"` (Raviart-Thomas, face flux) velocity basis to `SolverImplicitMPM` for inviscid fluids, which advects particles in a velocity field that is divergence-free at every point after an incompressible `"P0"` solve. With `collider_basis="RT1"`, colliders hold the face fluxes through them and the strain counts only the open part of cut cells, which keeps water at rest in tanks whose walls cut through the grid.
